## Meaning of milestone and task

- **Milestone** = demonstrable verified end result (e.g., Admin approval recorded and app status changes). It becomes complete only when all required tasks, tests, PR reviews and evidence are accepted.
- **Task** = one actionable deliverable, linked to one new milestone, one full-stack primary owner and a layer. When a legacy milestone covered two members, its seven tasks are allocated individually; never blindly transfer all seven.
- **Original tracker and deadlines** remain historical reference, not authoritative after rebaseline until 7 members approve assignments.
